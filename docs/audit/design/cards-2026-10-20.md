# Design audit: cards against the approved card design (2026-10-20)

By an independent agent; mockup docs/design/cards-lean.html, 849 live cards at 23:56 UTC on 19 Oct. Screenshots stayed in the session scratchpad.

## Verdict

The redesign components exist in `web/src/components/AnnouncementCard.tsx`: family colours, BlockShell, DealBlock, RatingBlock, CaseBlock, bank and insurer lens rows, decision lines and the ⋯ menu. **On real data, almost no card shows a family block.** Three reasons:

1. **The pipeline fills the blocks only when a filing is analysed again** (`pipeline.py` around line 2631, commit efbe96b, 19 Oct).
   - Cards made before that have no `scale`, `rating_change`, `case_stage` or `guidance_recall`.
   - Their `results` has no `lens` or `lines`.
   - Only **14 of 849** cards carry these keys.
   - No command recomputes these code-only blocks without calling the AI again.
2. **Three surfaces still draw the old card** through `components/FilingCard.tsx` (`FilingRow` / `FilingBody`):
   - the List view (`&view=list`), expanded row;
   - the Results page, row expansion;
   - the Today page, low-impact rows.

   That old card has "Core catalyst", AI "[Filing]" prose on the face, "Why it matters · memory & forensics" and a "Financial footer" with 1W/1M/1Y. It has no family colour and no block, and it breaks D38. See `real-list-view-card-d-light.png` and `real-results-page-expanded-d-light.png`.
3. **Where data exists, parts of the mockup are unbuilt or worse than the mockup.**
   - Not built: scope, the verdict chip, the titled "For the decision" box, money at stake on a case, and issue price / discount / dilution for capital deals.
   - Worse: the results column order, sign colouring on bank lines, and the order ladder labels.

## 2. Repeats the mockup removed that still show on real cards

1. **The headline again as the first point.**
   - In 136 sampled cards, **59** face points re-use a headline figure. `repeats()` needs all figures plus ≥40% word overlap, so a reworded restatement passes.
   - Examples: Capri "up to ₹4,000 Cr"; HDFC Life concall "VNB ₹2,037 Cr, up 12%"; Heritage "19% YoY to ₹1,320 Cr"; NLC BESS; RBL amalgamation; RSWM 40.5%.
2. **The same figure three times.**
   - The scale line repeats the headline amount on 12 sampled cards. Jubilant: "35% of net worth" in the headline and in the box.
   - HDFC Life concall: ₹2,037 Cr / 12% in the headline, a key tile and point 1, plus a "VNB margin 25.0%" chip beside the 24.7% tile.
   - Novartis annual report: headline figures repeated in the tiles; contingent liabilities ₹305.45 Cr in a tile and a point.
   - CSB business update: ₹46,889 Cr / 18% in the headline, the volume strip and a point.
   - Newgen: a point restates the block's Value and Term tiles.
3. **1W twice.** Fixed on the card; still twice in List view and on the Results page.
4. **Impact said three ways.** Fixed on the card.
5. **Theme chips.** Fixed.
6. **Results said twice.** UCO's points restate operating profit and provisions, because the bank-lens rows are missing. One point reads "₹807.67 Cr,." with stray punctuation from the pipeline.
7. **Four buttons to one place.** Fixed on the card; List view and Results still have three buttons.
8. **Full analysis starts over.**
   - Key numbers repeats the table (UCO EPS).
   - The AI reading repeats the face's "Promise delivered" signal (UCO advances +24.7%).
   - "More from the filing" can repeat a face point.

Also:
- Footer KPI chips on every family (791 cards), which the lean mockup has on none of its non-results cards.
- A duplicate LOA shown as two cards: Magellanic/Provigil ₹29.13 Cr, ids `0f2061e4` and `2bc2ef91`.

## 3. The ten most important fixes, in order

1. **Pipeline: a no-AI `refresh-card-blocks` command** (in `cli.py`, next to `refresh-summaries`).
   - For each stored card: load the dossier; take `before` / `current` with `dossier_as_of`, as `pipeline.py` does around line 2606.
   - Recompute `results` (lens and lines), `scale`, `rating_change`, `case_stage` and `guidance_recall` from the stored `deal_value`, headline and points.
   - Write `card_json` back, locally and to the live database.
   - Today 835 of 849 cards lack these keys. This makes the redesign visible without an AI request.
2. **Web: List view, the Results-page expansion and Today's low-impact rows render `AnnouncementCard`** instead of `FilingRow` / `FilingBody`.
   - Files: `views/FeedView.tsx` lines 172 and 229, `views/ResultsView.tsx`, `views/TodayView.tsx` line 146, `components/FilingCard.tsx`.
3. **Web: draw the deal block without a stated amount** (counterparty, segment or stage, with "Value: not disclosed"), and **add Scope from `deal_value.segment`**.
   - In `AnnouncementCard.tsx`, `dealBlock` / `DealBlock`. This covers the 141 deal cards with no block.
4. **Web: fix `ResultsTable`.**
   - Column order: quarter, year ago, YoY, previous quarter, QoQ.
   - Lower-is-better colours for NPA, provisions, cost / combined ratio and claims.
   - Render `yoy_turn` / `qoq_turn` as on the Results page.
   - Drop rows that are all zeros.
5. **Pipeline: split M&A `counterparty` into `target` and `seller`.**
   - Until then, label it "Counterparty" in `COUNTERPARTY` (60 cards; Ceigall shows the seller as "Target").
6. **Web: blocks as in the mockup.**
   - "For the decision" as a family-tinted box with that heading.
   - Results table inside `BlockShell` ("Quarter in ₹ Cr" / "Bank lens · ₹ Cr") with the verdict chip from `ResultsView`.
7. **Web: stronger repeat rules.**
   - `cardText.repeats`: hide a point when ≥2 of its figures are in the headline or a block, even if the words differ.
   - Feed table, tile and block values into `faceText` / `blockText`.
   - `showsFigure`: strip "per share", "bps" and "x".
   - Drop key tiles whose value is in the headline; drop the SCALE line when it repeats the headline amount.
   - `moreFacts = unique.filter(verifiedFact).slice(factLimit)`.
8. **Web: chips and tags.**
   - Show footer KPI chips on results-family cards only (`footerMetrics`); drop "D/E 0.00x".
   - Show the provisional-stage meta tag only on ord/cap cards (34 cards).
9. **Pipeline plus web: the data the mockup needs and nothing stores.**
   - `case_stage.at_stake` (through `scale_parts`'s maths) and `case_stage.subject`.
   - `deal_value` price, discount, dilution and swap ratio, drawn as DealBlock rows and a dilution bar.
10. **Web: ladder and block details.**
    - Relabel the ladder's first step only when `at === 0` (16 cards read "Contract, Contract…"); date the current step.
    - "Value, ex GST"; the counterparty in the block title.
    - Rating title with the rated amount, and "outlook ↓".
    - "USFDA" family label; VolumeStrip and KeyTiles inside the family BlockShell.
    - Then the results table for annual reports (from `annual_xbrl`) and for concalls (pipeline line 2627, plus the web `table` condition).

**Pipeline fixes:** 1, 5, 9, the annual-report / concall results in 10, the duplicate LOA cards and the stray "Cr,." punctuation.
**Web fixes:** 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10.
Fix 1 alone takes the share of cards carrying the new blocks from 14 of 849 to most of the 849. Fix 2 removes the old layout the owner still sees in List view and on the Results page.
