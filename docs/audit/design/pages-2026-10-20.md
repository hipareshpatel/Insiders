### Verdict
- **The tokens are an exact copy of the mockup.** That covers canvas, surface colours, radius 22, the shadows, Manrope and JetBrains Mono, and both dark themes (`web/src/index.css`).
- **The home page's components are a faithful port, but the page does not look approved, for three reasons:**
  1. **The data never triggers the mockup's best parts.** Only 3 of 850 published cards are HIGH (0.35%, where the mockup assumes about 16%), and only 2 cards ever passed all four gates. So the signals strip, red story rings, the gradient "N that could move a stock" clause, the High impact tab, red chart dots, the radar and overdue promises never appear.
     - The radar returns 0 companies and `/api/promises/overdue` returns [].
     - 0 of 85 companies have technicals and 0 are starred.
     - The default "Since close" window showed 0 cards at 05:15 IST. The first view is six zeros and an empty dashed box, on the deployed site too.
     - `/api/system` has no runs, with 561–790 filings queued.
  2. **The chrome and two pages were never restyled.** These are the top bar, `/feed` (old underline tabs, narrow off-centre column) and the Companies table (every cell wraps).
  3. **The company page was built to a different layout, with text-heavy sections.**
     - The price chart is full width above the tiles.
     - Good & bad and What to track are long lists of raw machine text.
     - There is no "Cards" panel.
     - The Filings tab is 43 full cards, 28,671 px tall.
     - At phone width the page is 6,627 px against the mockup's 4,194.
- **SPEC section 10 was rewritten to describe the build, so it cannot settle disputes; the mockup has to.** For example, it lists revenue and 1-year return where the mockup has P/E and 52-week range, and it never says where the price chart goes.
- **Deployed against localhost:** the page layout is identical; only the card internals differ.
  - The deployed cards still show "Core catalyst" and memory lines, with dates like "16 Oct, 21:17".
  - The local build drops "Core catalyst", adds an "AI reading" fold and prints "16 Oct 2026".
  - The card audit should confirm that the local build lost "Core catalyst".

### 3. Other pages against SPEC section 10
- **`/feed`: HIGH.** It still looks like the old site.
  - Underline tabs instead of pills, and the title "Live filings".
  - The card column is 720 px and offset to the right.
  - Its own rail ("3 filed / 1 analysed / 1 noise") differs from the home rail.
  - 50 a page and the List toggle are present.
- **Companies: MED.**
  - Market cap, size and date wrap to 3 lines.
  - Every row says "0 open · 0 capex" because the API returns null `open_promises` and `active_capex` (Heritage has 11 open).
  - Credibility is mostly "—", and sizes read "Midcap" where elsewhere they read "Mid cap".
- **Signals: MED, NO DATA.** An empty state only, and KPI labels that differ from the home page's.
- **Results, Calendar and System match the spec.** The calendar agenda shows raw filing text.
- **Navigation follows the spec.** It shows System and puts the search box only at ≥ 1,280 px, which differs from the mockup.

### 4. Visual language
- **Canvas, colours, card depth, radius and fonts match exactly.** One exception: the company price chart turns red or green by the year's move, where the mockup uses indigo. A red area chart on most company pages reads as alarm.
- **The header was not restyled:**
  - It is 56 px tall against 62.
  - The logo is the old chart SVG plus "NSE", not the gradient "In" tile.
  - The active nav item uses accent-soft blue where the mockup uses a neutral pill; nav weight is 500 against 600.
  - Search is a white bordered 240 px box reading "Search companies ⌘K", shown only from 1,280 px.
  - The theme control is a monitor icon.
- **The container is 1,200 px against 1,240**, so every column is about 90 px narrower.
- **Density is right on home but far too high on the company page.**
- **Dark mode is fine.** Only the grey low-impact dots are nearly invisible on the dark chart.
- **Phone width:** no horizontal scroll anywhere, but the company page is 58% taller than the mockup.
- **Data-driven look:** the mockup's colour comes from HIGH cards and signals. With 0.35% HIGH, the site is amber and grey and looks flat even where the code is right.

### 5. The ten most important fixes, in order
1. **Never open on an empty home page.** When the window has 0 cards, fall back to the last session's cards or default to Today or 7 days. Show the latest signals whatever the window. Get the pipeline running (no runs, 790 queued). Where: `TodayView.tsx` `startOf()`, the range default and `SignalsStrip`.
2. **Recalibrate impact and the four gates.** 3 HIGH and 2 signals in 850 cards means the signals strip, red rings, red dots and gradient headline never render. Until that is fixed, colour rings and dots by score band. Where: pipeline scoring; `Stories`, the `IMPACT_DOT` colours in `PriceChart` and the dots in `Timeline`.
3. **Restyle the top bar to the mockup and widen the container to 1,240 px.**
   - 62 px bar with the gradient "In" logo tile.
   - Neutral surface-2 active pill at weight 600.
   - A filled 340 px search reading "Search company, symbol or event", visible from about 1,024 px.
   - Where: `components/TopNav.tsx` and every `max-w-[1200px]` wrapper in the views.
4. **Rebuild the company Overview to the approved order.** Tiles first, then two columns: left Price, Story so far, Good & bad, What to track, Cards; right Promises, Orders, Notes, Coming up. Move "How the story is unfolding" to the Timeline tab or into the Cards panel. Where: the overview branch of `CompanyPage.tsx` (about lines 113–118) and `Overview`.
5. **Good & bad as tinted panels.** Use pos-soft and neg-soft backgrounds, at most 3 short items each with a source line ("Q2FY27 results · 15 Oct"). Strip machine tokens and impossible bps values in the client and at source. Where: `GoodBad` and the good/bad builder; pipeline signal text.
6. **What to track as 4 tiles:** KPI to verify, Guidance, Pending, Road blocker, the newest of each, linking to the tracker. Where: the `tracking` array in `Overview`, replacing `TrackList`.
7. **Add the Cards panel and make the Filings tab a compact list.** Rows on an impact-dot rail with chips (impact, family, T+1/T+5), All/High/Results/Deals filters, a pager, and each row expanding to the full card. Where: the filings branch of `CompanyPage.tsx` and a new Overview panel, reusing `FilingRow`.
8. **"The story so far" from memory.** A 2–3 sentence narrative with highlighted figures and "from N filings, updated <date>". This needs a dossier field from the memory step; until then, compose it on the client from the results streak, latest deal and promise record. Where: pipeline memory; "The story" in `Overview`.
9. **Company header and chart to the mockup.**
   - Make Watch a primary button.
   - Show the day's change "▲ x (y%) today" and two-decimal prices.
   - Populate technicals (0 of 85 companies have them) so the 52-week and DMA tags appear.
   - Bring back P/E (TTM) and the 52-week range (computable from `chart`).
   - Draw the price line in indigo with month ticks and a 3Y range, and keep dot clicks on the page.
   - Where: the header and `KeyFigures` in `CompanyPage`; `PriceChart.tsx`; the market-data job.
10. **Bring `/feed` and the rail's data in line with home.**
    - Restyle `/feed` with pill tabs, the home `Filters`, a centred card column and the home rail.
    - Fix `/api/promises/overdue`, which returns [] while company trackers show overdue items.
    - Fix the null `open_promises`/`active_capex` that make Companies read "0 open · 0 capex" everywhere.
    - Make the footer health line work.
    - Add per-family counts to `/api/brief`, the "More since the close" compact block, and watchlist sparklines.
    - Where: `FeedView.tsx`, `Rail` and `Health` in `TodayView`, `web/worker/public.ts`, and the column widths in `CompaniesView.tsx`.
