- **Owners' profit overwritten with total profit, or comprehensive income read as profit:** Bajaj Finance (two cards), DB Corp, NIIT.
- **Code-built figures that mean nothing:** EBITDA for lenders and insurers (Bajaj Finserv, IRFC), a deal size of shares x face value (Torrent), SCALE signals on them; the out_of_fit rule flagged only true figures (exceptional gains, loss-makers).

## What happens next, in order

1. Stop our fixers making cards wrong: the unit fixer may only change a figure the filing prints at that unit (never a right one); the change fixer may not replace a change the filing states; owners' profit never overwritten. Each with tests on these cards, on/off over stored cards.
2. The commonest false holds, each with the on/off and an independent sample (they make a check accept more): computed figures (a sum, a difference, a ratio of figures the card or filing has), figures in another unit (lakh, million, billion, Indian grouping), table rows the text layer splits, and the out_of_fit rule on exceptional quarters.
3. Release the cards marked RELEASE, and the RELEASE_WITHOUT ones with their named points dropped, through the release path (`release-held`), never by editing stored data; KEEP_HELD cards re-run after the fixes.
