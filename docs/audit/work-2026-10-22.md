The rule as applied (owner, 09:12 IST: "feel free to decide the rules ... take correct steps only"):
- **Right:** a reading, comparison or computed figure that follows from the filing and the memory the model was given,
  even when the filing does not say it in those words (a margin gap from two stated margins, a share of revenue).
- **Rounding is not an error** while it keeps the figure's meaning at the precision shown: ₹1,023.4 Cr as ₹1,023 Cr,
  14.83% as 14.8%. It is still an error when rounding changes what the reader concludes: a 0.4% decline shown as 0%,
  a loss shown as break-even, a rating notch or a basis-point move lost.
- **Still wrong, AI or not:** a figure that is not in or computable from what the model was given, a unit or scale
  slip (lakh as crore, US$ million as ₹ crore), a wrong period or basis, a cause the sources do not give, and a
  direction (positive / negative for shareholders) the facts contradict. These are the investor-harm errors the gate
  exists for.
