## What each card type has

**Call card (01-03).**
- Header: company, "Q2 FY27 EARNINGS CALL", date. A results verdict chip ("Q2 RESULT Fantastic / Good / Neutral").
- Four tiles: Overall (Strongly positive / Neutral), New guidance (Maintained / Raised / Cut), Last guidance (Met / Missed, coloured), and one key figure (revenue with YoY and QoQ, or order book with its date).
- 5-6 bullets. Each has a bold lead with the figure ("FY27 capex guidance kept at ₹500 Cr:"), then one or two sentences: who said it (the MD, the CFO by name, or "an analyst asked... management clarified"), and the comparison with last call ("same as mentioned in last call").
- What the card covers:
  - Corrections and one-offs ("₹60 Cr misclassified")
  - Working capital and cash flow, with the before figure
  - New businesses with a size ("data centre ₹350-450 Cr")
  - Questions management deflected
- "What to watch": dated items (commissioning by Nov 2026, Mar 2027).
- Chips: Tone, Transparency, Story.

**Results card (05, 07, 08, 10).**
- A result score with the last quarter's score, on a -10 to +10 scale.
- The quarterly P&L table: this quarter, previous quarter, year ago, YoY and QoQ. Rows: revenue, EBITDA, margin in basis points, PBT, PAT. **No EPS row: owner rule, 26 Oct 2026; the examples show EPS and the rule wins.**
- "AI decode": the movers with their size. Example (08): "operating profit up 21.58%... absorbed by ₹11.86 Cr depreciation and ₹2.59 Cr finance costs". Then cash flow and working capital, inventory build and its basis-point effect on margin, and the outlook.
- Growth catalyst and road blocker.
- Guidance tracker: the target, this result against it, and a status (On track).

**Event card (04, 06, 09 and 12-33).**
- Ticker, market cap and size band. Impact chip (High / Moderate). Category chips.
- The headline carries the number.
- 2-3 bullets of what the filing says, with key figures in bold.
- A "Why it matters" box, built from memory:
  - The size against the company: "about 13% of yearly sales", "over half its last quarter sales".
  - How it links to earlier filings: "fourth transmission win in a month", "clears the ₹25 Cr expansion approved in May 2026", "completes the expansion flagged in its annual report".
  - What decides the outcome: "subscription levels decide...", "tender allocations decide...", "the filing does not name the buyer".
- The footer: last revenue, and 1W, 1M and 1Y price moves.

## Rules these examples confirm
- Every figure is from the filing or from memory, never invented. Example: Sikko's 1,65,500 MT and USD 22.99 million must be on the filing's annexure, and a figure that is not is cut.
- Compare with the past from memory: last call, last quarter, an earlier filing with its date.
- Name what the filing does not say, when that decides the effect ("the filing does not disclose expected sales").
- Plain words, one idea a bullet, bold for the figure that matters.
