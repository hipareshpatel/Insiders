Making the information worth a decision

Have the data now

  • Price and market cap since the filing: stored at filing time, shown in Full analysis today; moves to the card face for any card older than a day
  • Run-rate valuation from the card's own profit and the market cap, marked as computed
  • "Next check" from the card's track items, on the face instead of only in Full analysis
  • The headline leads with profit and the sector's own measure (asset quality for a bank), not the top line

Needs a pipeline change (small)

  • Bank results table: the banking XBRL we already download carries gross and net NPA (amount and %) and return on assets; we map only interest earned, interest expended, operating profit and provisions. Measured on the bank XBRL in our tests (542867, Q3FY25).
  • Net interest income = interest earned − interest expended, both already stored
  • YoY and QoQ for every row from the same file's prior columns and memory, as the commercial table does
  • Insights that compare to memory must name the comparison ("highest in 6 quarters" only when it is the point)

Not in the XBRL (check before building)

  • Deposits, advances, CASA and NIM: assumption, they are in the press release or investor presentation, not the results XBRL; to be checked on PSB's NSE file
  • Capital adequacy: the template has CET1 and Additional Tier 1 tags; whether PSB fills them is to be checked

The rules behind the lean card

Stays on the card

  • Company, price and 1-week move, once, in the header
  • One meta line: score and impact as one badge, direction, and the event in its family colour
  • A block that fits the event: results table, deal stages and size gauge, rating ladder, case ladder
  • Headline, then only points that add something to it
  • Why it matters, the computed scale and "Not disclosed"
  • The results table on results cards
  • Time, session, verified status, Full analysis, PDF

Moves into Full analysis or ⋯

  • 1M and 1Y returns (next to the market reaction already there)
  • Theme chips (they remain the tabs and filters)
  • Credibility, thin liquidity and price-trend chips, unless notable: credibility under 50%, liquidity thin, a Stage 2 trend
  • Copy as image, verify against the PDF and company memory go under ⋯; the company name still opens memory
  • "5d ago" becomes a tooltip on the time
  • Full analysis drops its repeated title line and the "Key numbers" already on the face

Dropped as a repeat

  • A point whose figures are all already in the headline
  • A signal line whose figure is in the headline, a point or the results table
  • A footer figure that the text already states
  • The coloured top bar (the badge carries impact)
  • At the source too: the card prompt will say the points must not restate the headline, so the model writes the new detail ("was CARE A (Stable)") instead

Concept. Demo companies use illustrative figures; the Punjab & Sind Bank card uses only the figures on your live card, and "xbrl" or "computed" marks a cell the pipeline would fill. Use the button at the top to hide the repeat markers.
