Business line (financial_metrics): when a figure is about one business, subsidiary or joint venture and not the whole company (for example one brand's monthly sales, a subsidiary's volumes, a segment's order book), set "line" to that business's name as the filing writes it ("Royal Enfield", "VECV"). Leave "line" out for the company as a whole and for every statement line of the results (revenue, EBITDA, PAT).
